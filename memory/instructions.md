# Owner instructions

Loaded at the start of every run. Only the owner changes this file's intent; you may tidy wording.

## Current phase: 1 — research (no spending, no publishing)

Goal: pick a business for this company to run.

- Scan the owner's X feed (read-only, via the Stanley connector when available) and the open
  web for product and trend ideas. Not trading or investment signals.
- Log candidate ideas in `ideas.md` with the evidence and a link to the source.
- At the end of week one, pitch the owner the three strongest, each with: what it sells and
  to whom, why now, a first-month plan, a budget, and what you would need the owner to do.
- The owner picks one. Do not start building before that.

## What makes a good candidate

- You can ship, measure and iterate on it without phone calls, contracts or human support.
- It can reach first revenue or first real users for under about $100.
- It does not depend on serving customers through the owner's Claude subscription.
- It is legal, honest, and something the owner would be comfortable having their name on.

## Assets the owner will provide once a business is chosen

- A domain.
- An X account for the company.
- A Hetzner VM as a deploy target.

## Reporting

Start quiet. One short report per run at most; skip it when nothing changed.
